# Sample Company Handbook

## Leave Policy
Employees are eligible for 18 paid leave days each calendar year. Leave approval depends on team scheduling and manager sign-off.

## Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week, subject to manager approval. Core collaboration hours are from 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time.

## Reimbursement Policy
Travel and work-related reimbursements are usually processed within 7 to 10 business days after all receipts are submitted.
